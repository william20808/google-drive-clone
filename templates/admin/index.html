How to Change User Storage Limits & Tiers

1
Open Storage Plans: Go to User Profiles & Storage Plans.
2
Inline Dropdown: Select 15 GB, 100 GB, or 500 GB directly on the user's table row, then click Save at the bottom.
3
Bulk Upgrades: Or check multiple users, select "⚡ Upgrade storage to 100 GB / 500 GB" from the Action bar, and click Run.
